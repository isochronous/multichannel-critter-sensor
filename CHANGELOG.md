# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed

- New building art by 3GuB, commissioned for this mod. The sensor's lights blink when it first notices something it tracks, and each channel has its own indicator: green lights and a slowly pulsing paw print for critters, green lights and a pulsing egg for eggs (the two pulse out of step). Red lights mean that channel's signal is off.

## 0.2.1 - 2026-09-23

### Fixed

- A placed but unbuilt sensor now shows the usual white construction outline instead of looking already built.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-09-20

- Animated art: paw prints cross the visor, an egg rocks on the right, and each antenna glows green while its signal is on.
- The mod's logo is the Workshop preview.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-20

- First release: a room-based critter and egg counter that outputs several signals over one automation ribbon.
